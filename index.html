My name is Hao Liu (刘豪). I am a researcher working on software and firmware security, with a focus on vulnerability detection for the Internet of Things and Industrial Internet of Things. My research explores how program representations and machine learning can support the analysis of source code and firmware binaries.

My research interests include IoT and IIoT security, source-code and binary analysis, large language models for software security, and cross-architecture vulnerability retrieval.
