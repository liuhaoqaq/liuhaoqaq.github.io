My name is Hao Liu. I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Southeast University, supervised by Prof. Liquan Chen, and a joint Ph.D. student in Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Gerhard P. Hancke. My research focuses on IoT and industrial IoT security, firmware vulnerability detection, binary analysis, large language models for code, and post-quantum cryptography.

🔥 News

  • Our paper on source-code vulnerability detection in IoT firmware was accepted by ICICS 2026.
  • Our survey on semantic firmware bugs and security challenges was accepted by Frontiers of Computer Science.
